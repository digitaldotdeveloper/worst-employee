THE POINT

Hit something hard enough and it becomes chaotic. If it then hits something else, the chain continues. Coffee → monitor → printer → papers → coworker → boss. Longer chain, bigger multiplier.

Productivity going down is the win condition. Fill the boss's anger bar to trigger the fight.

Wrecking the place is optional. Walking past things does not break them — you have to actually hit, throw or chain them. Coast through a whole shift doing nothing and you get paid for that too, under a different rank.

THE OFFICE

Five rooms — reception, open plan, break room, meeting room and the boss's office. Walk through the doorways. Every room has people in it and people can be pestered: stand next to someone and press GRAB. It costs the company real productivity and winds the boss up, and it breaks absolutely nothing.

EVENTS

The shift interrupts itself. HR INSPECTION pays you to stand perfectly still. FIRE DRILL means nobody is watching. BIG CLIENT makes every bit of chaos cost double. A BUDGET CUT turns half the lights off.
